# Mimi Pastry

A responsive, multi-page bakery website for Mimi Pastry, Kaduna, Nigeria.

## Pages
- Home
- Wedding & Birthday Cakes
- Pastries & Drinks
- Our Story
- Contact & Orders

## Run locally
This is a static HTML/CSS/JavaScript website. Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder with a local static server.

## Before launch
- Confirm the business email spelling (currently entered as `goodessbeauty@gmail.com`, exactly as originally supplied).
- Replace the temporary bakery photography with approved Mimi Pastry product photos.
- Add the Miracode portfolio URL to the footer credit in `assets/js/app.js`.
- Verify phone, WhatsApp ordering, location, and all contact details with the business owner.

No prices are displayed; visitors are directed to WhatsApp for quotes.
